Notebooks

Group your pages, like OneNote. Each note lives in one notebook. Actions, risks, issues and decisions still roll up everywhere, and you can filter them by notebook. Deleting a notebook moves its pages to another one.

People

Anyone you assign actions to. Typing “@Sam” in an action adds Sam here by itself. Removing someone just unassigns their actions.

Categories

Group your items and actions. Deleting a category just un-categorises things; nothing else is removed.

⚡ Quick add

Type it the way you'd say it, e.g. “call Sam Friday 3pm” or “timecards every other Friday”. Start with note: to make a note instead, or risk:, issue: or decision: for the RAID log.

⌨ Keyboard shortcuts & tips
