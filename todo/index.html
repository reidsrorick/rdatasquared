Appearance

Team and assigned-action feeds

Your team is the list of people you can assign to. A feed is a file in a folder you choose for that person (share that folder with them) listing the actions assigned to them, so they can see them and answer “done?”. You always confirm. It holds each action’s text, date, notes and status, not your notes.

From the team

If someone shared a folder with you, connect it here. Then you see what is assigned to you and the pages shared with you, and can send “done?” requests and suggestions back.

Help

RAID types

Choose the types this notebook uses, what you call them, and add your own or delete the ones you do not need (at least one stays). It is saved in this notebook’s file, so your work and home notebooks can differ. Switching one off hides its page, menus and shortcuts; the items you already have stay in your notes.

Notifications

Quick access toolbar

A slim strip above your notes with the buttons you use most. Tick the ones you want and use the arrows to put them in order. It is saved in your notebook file.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press Change, then the keys you want. Keys without Ctrl or Alt (like N or /) only work when you are not typing; Ctrl or Alt combinations work anywhere. Saved in your notebook file, so they follow you between computers.

Replies

Nothing here changes your page until you accept it. Names come from the reply file and are not verified.

My Notebook

Compare this copy with your live site, and see what changed in each version.

Notebooks

Group your pages, like OneNote. Use ▲ ▼ (or drag a heading in the Notes list) to put the notebooks in the order you like. Each note lives in one notebook. Actions, risks, issues and decisions still roll up everywhere, and you can filter them by notebook. Deleting a notebook moves its pages to another one.

People

Anyone you assign actions to. Typing “@Sam” in an action adds Sam here by itself. Removing someone just unassigns their actions.

Categories

Group your items and actions. Deleting a category just un-categorises things; nothing else is removed.
